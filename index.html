About the Role

Someone to own the coordination so you can own the closings

Transworld Business Advisors of Western Carolinas helps people buy and sell businesses across the region. It's high-touch, relationship-heavy work where every deal has moving parts: buyers to screen, paperwork to track, follow-ups to manage, and outreach to run. That coordination layer is what keeps deals from stalling.

Right now, Eli is handling that coordination alongside everything else. With four kids, another on the way, and a full pipeline of deals, the admin side is eating into time that should go to closings and family. The need here is clear: a Lead Coordinator who can take ownership of the day-to-day, screen potential buyers before they hit Eli's calendar, handle the administrative backbone of the operation, and potentially run cold outreach to keep the pipeline warm.

We sourced five candidates within budget, each with a different angle on the role. Some lean heavier into admin and operations. Others bring outreach and sales muscle. A couple bring client success and relationship management experience that translates directly to a brokerage environment. All of them can work EST hours and hit the ground running.
